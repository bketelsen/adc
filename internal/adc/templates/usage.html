How these counts are reported

Time windows use the date ADC received a report. Counts may omit unreported calls. Cache counts are separate and are not added to input or output. “Unknown” means no valid count was received. “Partial” means some reports or observed runs have no count. Runs without reports cannot be assigned to an actual model. Cache-write reporting began with this release; older reports remain unknown.
