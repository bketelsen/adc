# Completion that fits the responsibility

Areas have a human-selected completion mode. Reviewed is the default and preserves the existing engineering contract. Routine permits the accountable owner to finish ordinary non-code work on recorded observed evidence, without inventing a separate QA document or delegation. These are two small named policies, not a workflow language or a hard-coded list of domains.

A new assignment can select an area; proposed work can retain or select one, and its acceptance displays the applicable mode. The assignment saves a versioned policy when it starts. Area edits affect new work, not existing assignments or their later verification obligations. Nil on historical work always means reviewed. Old schedule templates do not acquire new area defaults, and follow-up funding copies the originating work's policy. A routine policy changes completion checks, never authority, credentials, access, publication or membership.

Registered code artifacts and execution-plan steps require independent model-family review under either policy. Bounded recurring owner assessments retain their independently reviewed area observations and briefing. Ordinary routine notes and documents do not trigger artificial QA. Optional expert review remains available.

`adc_evidence` stores bounded observed completion evidence with a source reference and revision. A routine owner may record it directly; reviewed work uses a substantive specialist. References are labels, not fetch instructions: one line, no credentials, URL query or fragment. Updated evidence participates in review pins. Cancelled, superseded or unfinished worker evidence cannot satisfy completion. Generic evidence does not replace a linked obligation's `adc_obligation_result`: that record must exist before a verification assignment finishes, and the saved policy determines its review requirement. Failed observed verification can finish the assessment while the owed outcome remains blocked.

The Areas form selects the policy, the assignment form selects responsibility, and task metadata shows the saved policy. Proposal refinement can change the selected area before acceptance. Changing a recurring assessment proposal explicitly to one-off removes its recurrence policy while keeping it pending; editing numeric attention limits retains the earlier proposal revision unchanged.

`adc_status` offers focused summary, run, review, assessment, connections, proposals and document views so coordination need not repeatedly consume the whole snapshot. Empty View keeps the legacy full response. Focused run reads stay inside the same assignment and organization. Already-finished child work is a normal `adc_wait` response; the provider yields only if a wait actually changed run state. Candidate-review tools are offered only to planned workers, and routine-owner evidence tools are not offered to reviewed supervisors or reviewers who cannot use them.

Qualification separates two questions: whether an activity finished and whether its wider outcome is verified. An experimental graphics milestone can finish under review while hardware validation remains owed. An external tester's claim cannot directly record a result on an unrelated internal verification task or change human intent. This is not public-contribution admission security: the hostile-input boundary and publication route are P6 work.

The same core is exercised with a timed family confirmation, a NAS protection follow-up and a product/delivery investigation. Live fixtures use supplied synthetic evidence, not real family arrangements, infrastructure health or measured hosting costs. Existing production organizations remain reviewed unless a human deliberately changes an area's mode. No new permanent team or speculative maintenance is created by selecting a policy.
